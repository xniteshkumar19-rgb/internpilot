The Freshers' Problem

Predatory Scams

Fake postings demanding "registration fees", kit charges, or sensitive bank details before any interview.

Unclear Skill Fit

First-year students guessing whether their coursework qualifies them for competitive roles.

Scattered Deadlines

Application deadlines buried across chaotic WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, and scattered notes.

How the Agent Decides

  • 1. Ingest & Read Posting Google Gemini parses unstructured requirements under prompt injection isolation.
  • 2. Rule-Based Scam Check Deterministic rules check for upfront fees, free email domains, and pressure tactics. Halts immediately on high risk.
  • 3. Skill Alignment Match Token-boundary matching compares requirements to resume. Match < 30% suggests skills to learn first.
  • 4. Grounded Cover Email Drafts a concise (<150 words) email citing only facts from the candidate's resume.
  • 5. User Confirmation & Tracker Never auto-saves. Asks for explicit user approval before storing to localStorage.
